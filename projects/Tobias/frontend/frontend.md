## Notes
- In the task, it says that I should add desired ELO of opponent when creating a game. I decided to not implement this, because of 2 reasons. 1: the backend was made to autmatically handle ELO-based matchmaking, and I also found this a bit weird, because then users can just put a low desired ELO and play against beginners. I thought it would be more realistic and better if they are automatically paired up with players around their same level.
